ABOUT ADMINIVERSAL

Administration is the foundation.

We believe good business support is more than paperwork. It is about creating the structure that allows people, suppliers, clients and opportunities to connect.

ADMINIVERSAL is a South African business support and supply company focused on helping organisations and individuals access practical services, products and administrative support.

Our work brings together administration, sourcing, procurement, coordination and operational support. We listen to what a client needs, organise the requirements, identify appropriate solutions and coordinate the process from requirement to delivery.

This allows ADMINIVERSAL to participate in opportunities ranging from everyday business requirements to formal RFQs, tenders and procurement requests.

As the business grows, we also want our work to create practical exposure for young people and communities by connecting them with the skills required to support real client needs.

OUR APPROACH

Support the need. Build the capability.

Every client requirement is an opportunity to organise better, source responsibly, deliver effectively and develop people through meaningful work.

SUPPLY & PROCUREMENT

Your requirement. Our sourcing process.

ADMINIVERSAL participates in procurement opportunities as a supplier and service provider, helping clients source products and coordinate services around their actual requirements.

When an organisation needs something, finding the right product or service should not become an unnecessary administrative burden.

ADMINIVERSAL works to understand the requirement, identify suitable sourcing options, communicate with suppliers, obtain relevant quotations and coordinate the procurement process.

We can participate in RFQs, tenders and direct procurement opportunities where the required product or service falls within our supply capability.

Our approach considers more than price. We consider the client's specifications, quality, availability, supplier reliability, lead times and the overall requirement.

OUR SUPPLY MODEL

Requirement → Source → Coordinate → Deliver

We use administration to keep the procurement process organised from the initial requirement through to fulfilment.

01

Understand the Requirement

We establish exactly what the client requires, including specifications, quantities and timing.

02

Identify Options

We look for suitable products, suppliers or service providers that can meet the requirement.

03

Coordinate Procurement

04

Fulfil the Requirement

We coordinate the next steps toward supplying the required product or service to the client.
